## The news in the image

All items below were reported on Wednesday 18 November 2026 (Reuters world
news summary and same-day roundups). Nothing in the image is invented.

1. **Ukraine hits the Crimean bridge (lead story).** Ukrainian drones and
   missiles destroyed the central road section of the bridge linking Russia
   and Crimea; the bridge was shut. A military and symbolic blow to Putin
   two days before he is due to meet Xi and Trump.
   Source: Reuters world news summary, 18 Nov 2026 (via Devdiscourse);
   Jagran Josh 18 Nov headlines.
2. **Trump in Manila.** US President Donald Trump met Philippine President
   Ferdinand Marcos Jr. in Manila on Wednesday for Quad-linked talks and a
   working lunch, before heading to Shenzhen for Thursday's meeting with Xi
   at the APEC summit. Tuesday he held his first Quad leaders' meeting
   (US, Japan, India, Australia) on critical minerals, maritime security
   and AI.
   Source: Reuters, "Trump to meet Marcos in Manila before China talks",
   18 Nov 2026.
3. **US strikes Iranian launch site.** US fighter aircraft struck a missile
   launch site on Qeshm Island in retaliation; Iran had attacked a US
   military radar system in Bahrain with drones.
   Source: Reuters world news summary, 18 Nov 2026; Jagran Josh 18 Nov
   headlines.
4. **19 dead in Siberia plane crash.** A Russian military An-12 transport
   aircraft crashed in the Kemerovo region, killing all 19 on board.
   Source: Reuters world news summary, 18 Nov 2026.
5. **Canada and China extend trade deals.** Xi met Canadian PM Mark Carney
   ahead of the APEC summit.
   Source: Jagran Josh / Education Times 18 Nov headlines.
